Territory boundary colours

Territory tier borders

The territory border shows defence or treasury, chosen in Map controls. Medium is yellow, High is light red, and Very High is dark red. Grey means the tier is unknown. Dashed outlines are the default; Appearance can switch them to solid. Choose Guild to use the guild accent instead.

Live map: reported Wynncraft API tiers take priority. Local territory edits use your custom values. Treasury is calculated from hold time only when its reported tier is missing.

Defence score: damage + attack speed + health + damage resistance + aura + volley upgrade levels. Subtract 5 when aura is 0; subtract 3 when volley is 0. The four tower upgrades range from 0–11; aura and volley range from 0–3.

The defence calculator in Edit territory applies this model to a local plan. HQ advances the calculated defence tier by one, capped at Very High. Connections and nearby territories also affect tower stats.

Sources: Wynncraft API tier fields, Wynnmap tier thresholds and colours, Wynnmap defence-score model. These are Wynnmap's documented calculation rules; this tool uses reported game tiers for live state.
